# Concept & Vision

Plantasonic is a **living botanical dream** — a dark room breathing with bioluminescent specimens that respond to sound. It sits at the intersection of antique herbarium illustration and retro-futurist pixel art: hyper-detailed plants rendered as luminous, low-fidelity, dithered artifacts that feel both ancient and synthetic.

## Experience goals

- **Calm and hypnotic** by default; capable of blooming into high-energy peaks driven by audio.
- **Never frozen** — idle motion (sway, breathe, shimmer, drift) is always present.
- **Endlessly novel** — the AI Director and procedural variation ensure no two moments repeat.
- **Alive, slightly uncanny** — light emanates from *within* the plants.

## Why AI-first and data-driven

A traditional installation would ship a fixed library of rendered frames or images. Plantasonic instead ships **descriptions**: a Style DNA, asset metadata, and behavior/scene definitions. From those, the system generates and varies visuals on demand. This keeps the project:

- **Small** — no image dumps in the repo.
- **Reproducible** — same definitions + Style DNA → same assets.
- **Evolvable** — change the Style DNA and the entire installation re-skins itself.
- **Future-proof** — new AI models slot in behind the prompt builder.

## Aesthetic pillars

1. Internal emissive lighting over external light.
2. Indexed, dithered, low color count.
3. Centered specimens with generous negative space.
4. Organic texture that survives quantization.
5. Motion coupled to audio energy and mood.
