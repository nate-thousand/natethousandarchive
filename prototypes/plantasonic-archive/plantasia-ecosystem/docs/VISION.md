# Vision

## Plantasia is not a synthesizer.

It is a **living sound ecosystem**.

The user does not program an instrument — they **cultivate organisms** that
generate music through growth, evolution, mutation, and interaction. The
interface should feel more like tending a digital garden than operating a
machine. Every visual element communicates that the application is **alive**.

## What it should be, all at once

- **A musical instrument** — expressive, playable, deep.
- **A generative art installation** — beautiful, autonomous, never still.
- **An educational synthesis tool** — it teaches real synthesis through nature.
- **An ambient performance environment** — calm, immersive, atmospheric.

## Principles

1. **The ecosystem is the application.** The center organism dominates the
   interface; everything else frames it.
2. **Nothing ever stops moving.** Breathing, growing, drifting — life is
   continuous (while respecting reduced-motion).
3. **Nature over jargon.** Beginners meet Seeds, Sunlight, Water, and Wind —
   not oscillators, cutoff, and LFOs. Expert Mode reveals the machinery for
   those who want it.
4. **Every preset is a living species.** Species change visual structure,
   animation, sound engine, modulation, color, and behavior.
5. **Atmosphere is the aesthetic.** Minimal, organic, dark, monochromatic.
   Subtle bloom, very thin borders, soft gradients, fine grain, and generous
   negative space.
6. **Built to grow.** A scalable, domain-driven architecture so new species,
   engines, effects, and AI capabilities are additive — never rewrites.

## The feeling

Premium and calm. A botanical simulation with the flexibility of a modular
synthesizer, the beauty of a generative art piece, and the usability of a modern
design system.
