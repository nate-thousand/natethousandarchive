# Plantasia Product Definition v0.1

## Product Vision

Plantasia is a living audiovisual instrument inspired by the mathematics, behaviors, and growth systems of nature.

Rather than programming sounds through technical parameters, users cultivate musical ecosystems that grow, evolve, and bloom through interaction.

Plantasia transforms sound design into an act of exploration, meditation, and discovery where sound and visuals exist as one living organism.

The goal is not to imitate nature but to create an entirely new instrument category inspired by biological systems.

---

# Product Mission

Plantasia exists to create a completely original musical instrument where sound, interaction, and generative visuals become one expressive living system.

It is a sensory love letter to living systems.

---

# North Star

> It feels like growing music instead of making it.

Every design decision should reinforce this idea.

# Core Philosophy

Users do not program sounds.

Users cultivate living musical ecosystems.

Every interaction represents a biological process such as growth, branching, blooming, mutation, colonization, or emergence.

The interface should encourage cultivation rather than parameter editing.

---

# Product Principles

## Living System

Sound and visuals are not separate systems.

They are two expressions of the same organism.

There is no visualizer.

The organism is the instrument.

---

## Exploration Over Programming

Exploration should always feel rewarding.

The instrument should encourage curiosity rather than technical mastery.

---

## Meditation Before Production

Plantasia should create a calm creative state before becoming a production tool.

---

## Surprise Never Punish

The system should constantly introduce variation and discovery.

Every surprise should remain harmonic, musical, and beautiful.

---

## Progressive Disclosure

Beginners interact with biological concepts.

Advanced users can reveal the complete synthesis engine.

Nothing is hidden permanently.

Complexity is introduced naturally.

---

## Nature's Mathematics

Plantasia does not imitate plants.

It uses the mathematics and behaviors of nature.

Examples include:

* Fibonacci sequences
* Golden ratio
* Branching systems
* Cellular growth
* Mycelial networks
* Emergence
* Ecosystems
* Organic proportions

---

## Beauty Through Simple Rules

Complex forms should emerge from simple systems.

The interface should feel grown rather than designed.

# Visual Language

The primary experience is a procedural ASCII organism occupying most of the interface.

The organism continuously grows, branches, blooms, mutates, and evolves.

It is both the instrument and the visual representation of the current musical state.

Recognizable visual elements include:

* Procedural ASCII organisms
* Blooming geometric flowers
* Growing interconnected vines
* Flowing particle ecosystems
* Cellular structures
* Branching systems
* Harmonic wave structures
* Organic node networks
