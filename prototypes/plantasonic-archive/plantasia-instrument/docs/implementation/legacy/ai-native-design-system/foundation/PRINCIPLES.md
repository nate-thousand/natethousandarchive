# Principles

## Core Principle

Design OS is a methodology, not a folder of templates.

## Operating Principles

- Reuse decisions, not just files.
- Keep the framework lean enough to reach for naturally.
- Promote only what helps two or more unrelated products.
- Let real projects improve the framework.
- Prefer clear rules and templates over vague documentation.
- Accessibility is part of the design system, not a later pass.

## Anti-Principles

- Do not keep empty structure because it feels comprehensive.
- Do not add best-practice essays that do not change behavior.
- Do not promote product-specific flavor into the framework too early.
- Do not require documents that you would skip under pressure.
